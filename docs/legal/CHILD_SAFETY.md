# Child Safety

**Last Updated:** January 24, 2026

---

## Our Commitment

Protecting young people is one of our highest priorities. WeLynk is designed with safety in mind, especially for users under 18.

> **Zero Tolerance:** We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any form of child exploitation. We report all suspected cases to authorities.

---

## 1. Age Requirements

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Minimum age** | 16 years old |
| **Under 16** | Not allowed - accounts will be removed |
| **Ages 16-17** | Allowed with enhanced protections, separated from adults |
| **Ages 18+** | Adults only interact with other adults |
| **Age verification** | Required at registration |

### How Age Works on WeLynk

WeLynk doesn't create separate worlds for minors and adults. Most of the platform works the same for everyone - you can play games together, use the same features, and have the same experience.

For certain social matching features (chat matching, Truth or Dare, Would You Rather), we match minors with minors and adults with adults. This makes meeting new people safer and more relevant to your life.

### Adults Pretending to Be Minors

**This is one of the most serious violations on WeLynk.**

There is no legitimate reason for an adult to claim to be under 18. The only purpose would be to get matched with minors in social features. We treat this as predatory behavior.

If you are an adult who lies about your age to access minors:
- **Account banned** - no warnings, no appeals
- **Device banned** - every account associated with your device is removed from WeLynk
- **Law enforcement referral** - we will report you
- **Full legal action** - if any minor is harmed, we will pursue every legal remedy available

### Minors: Why Would You Lie?

If you're 16-17 and thinking about saying you're 18+... ask yourself why.

You already have access to everything. Same games. Same features. Same store. The only thing that changes is who you get matched with in social features - and being matched with people your age is actually better for you.

Lying about your age doesn't unlock secret adult content. There isn't any. All it does is change who you meet in social matching. When we find out, your account will be banned.

Age-based matching isn't a punishment. It's designed to make your experience better.

## 2. How We Protect Minors

### Automatic Protections for Users 16-17

When you're under 18, these protections are **always on** and can't be turned off:

- **Age-based matching:** Minors are only matched with other minors
- **Maximum content filtering:** Strictest safety filters active
- **Enhanced monitoring:** Interactions with adults are closely monitored
- **Stricter enforcement:** Violations affecting minors = harsher consequences

### Safety Systems

| System | What It Does |
|--------|--------------|
| **CSAM detection** | Automatically scans images for child abuse material |
| **Content filtering** | Blocks harmful content before you see it |
| **Predatory behavior detection** | Identifies grooming patterns and concerning behavior |
| **Human review** | Trained staff review flagged content and reports |

## 3. CSAM Detection & Reporting

When we detect potential child sexual abuse material:

1. **Immediate block** - Content is quarantined instantly
2. **Account suspended** - The user can't access WeLynk
3. **Review** - Our safety team examines the content
4. **Report to NCMEC** - We file a report with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children
5. **Law enforcement** - We preserve evidence and cooperate fully

This process is required by federal law (18 U.S.C. Section 2258A) and we take it very seriously.

## 4. Tips for Young Users

If you're 16-17, here's how to stay safe:

**Do:**
- Keep personal info private (real name, school, address, phone)
- Tell a trusted adult if someone makes you uncomfortable
- Use the report button when something feels wrong
- Trust your instincts

**Don't:**
- Share personal details with strangers
- Send photos you wouldn't want others to see
- Meet online friends in person without telling parents
- Keep concerning conversations secret

**Remember:** Adults who ask you to keep secrets, send photos, or meet privately are not your friends. Report them immediately.
