# Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** January 17, 2026
**Last Updated:** January 24, 2026

---

## The Short Version

WeLynk is a place to meet people, chat, and play games. To keep it fun and safe for everyone:

- **Be kind.** Treat others how you'd want to be treated.
- **Be real.** Don't pretend to be someone you're not.
- **Be safe.** Don't share harmful content or do sketchy things.
- **Be 16+.** You must be at least 16 years old to use WeLynk.

Break these rules and we'll take action - from warnings to permanent bans.

---

## 1. Who Can Use WeLynk

- **Minimum age:** 16 years old
- **If you're 16-17:** You get extra safety protections and can only be matched with other minors
- **If you're 18+:** You can only be matched with other adults

### How Age Works on WeLynk

Most of WeLynk works the same for everyone - minors and adults can play games together, use the same features, and enjoy the same experience.

For certain social matching features (chat matching, Truth or Dare, Would You Rather), we match people within their age group. This makes socializing safer and more relevant - not limited.

### What Chat Matching Is For

WeLynk's chat matching is designed for people who actually want to make friends or build genuine connections. If that turns into something more, cool - we're not here to tell you what to do.

But if you're spamming "M", "F", "ASL", or any of that garbage - we will ban you from matching. Keep doing it after warnings and we'll ban your account entirely. Seriously, there are a billion other websites for that. WeLynk isn't one of them. Go somewhere else. Don't make us waste time building systems to filter out this nonsense instead of building cool features everyone would love. You'd be ruining the fun for everyone.

**Note:** Most people here are just looking to make friends and play games, not to be weird. Even if you somehow get around our systems, you're not going to get the reaction you're hoping for - just reports. And then we'll catch you anyway.

### Adults Pretending to Be Minors

**Permanent ban. No exceptions.** There's no legitimate reason for an adult to claim to be under 18. The only purpose would be to get matched with minors in social features. We treat this as predatory behavior.

If we catch you:
- Your account is permanently banned
- Your device is banned - every account associated with it is removed from WeLynk
- We may report you to law enforcement

### Minors Claiming to Be Adults

If you're 16-17: why? You have access to everything already. Same games, same features, same store. The only difference is who you get matched with in social features - and honestly, you probably want to chat with people your age anyway.

Lying about your age doesn't unlock anything. When we find out, your account will be banned.

## 2. What's Not Allowed

### Content You Can't Post

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Child safety violations** | CSAM, exploitation of minors (zero tolerance - reported to authorities) |
| **Violence & threats** | Threats, glorifying violence, content promoting harm |
| **Hate speech** | Attacks based on race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, etc. |
| **Harassment** | Bullying, intimidation, targeted abuse |
| **Sexual content** | Nudity, explicit material, sexual solicitation |
| **Self-harm** | Content promoting or instructing self-harm or suicide |
| **Illegal activity** | Drug sales, fraud, anything illegal |
| **Spam** | Automated content, scams, manipulation |
| **IP violations** | Content you don't have rights to share |

### Behaviors That Get You Banned

| Behavior | What It Means |
|----------|---------------|
| **Impersonation** | Pretending to be someone else |
| **Fake accounts** | Creating multiple accounts to evade bans |
| **Predatory behavior** | Grooming or attempting to exploit minors |
| **Doxxing** | Sharing someone's private info without consent |
| **Ban evasion** | Coming back after being banned |
| **Hacking** | Exploiting bugs or trying to break the platform |

## 3. How We Enforce These Rules

### Automated Detection
We use automated systems to scan content for violations. This happens in real-time as you use the platform.

### Human Review
Humans review content when:
- Our systems flag something
- Someone reports it
- It involves serious violations

### What Happens If You Break the Rules

| Severity | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **Minor/first offense** | Warning, content removal |
| **Repeated violations** | Temporary suspension, feature restrictions |
| **Serious violations** | Permanent ban |
| **Illegal activity** | Permanent ban + reported to law enforcement |

We may skip straight to permanent bans for serious violations like CSAM, credible threats, or predatory behavior.

### Our Discretion
We decide what action to take based on:
- How serious the violation is
- Whether it's a pattern
- Impact on others
- Your account history

Our decisions are final, though you can appeal (see below).

## 4. Protecting Minors

Users aged 16-17 receive enhanced protections:

- **Matching restrictions:** Minors can only be matched with other minors
- **Maximum content filtering:** Strictest safety filters applied automatically
- **Adult-minor interactions:** Heavily monitored, stricter enforcement

### For Minor Users
- Never share personal info with strangers
- Tell a trusted adult about uncomfortable interactions
- Use the report button if something feels wrong
- Never meet online contacts without parental knowledge

### For Adults
Adults who engage inappropriately with minors face:
- Immediate permanent ban
- Referral to law enforcement
- No appeals

## 5. Child Safety (Zero Tolerance)

We have **zero tolerance** for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and child exploitation.

If we detect or receive reports of CSAM:
1. Content is immediately blocked
2. Account is suspended
3. We file a report with NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children)
4. Evidence is preserved for law enforcement
5. We cooperate fully with investigations

This is required by federal law (18 U.S.C. Section 2258A) and it's the right thing to do.

## 6. Reporting Problems

### How to Report
- **In the app:** Tap the report button on any profile, message, or content
- **By email:** support@welynk.com

### What to Include
- What happened
- Who was involved (username)
- When it happened
- Screenshots if you have them

### After You Report
- We'll review your report
- We may take action (we can't always tell you what due to privacy)

### Emergencies
**If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 first.** Then report to us.

## 7. Appeals

Think we made a mistake? You can appeal.

**How to appeal:** Email support@welynk.com with:
- Your username
- What decision you're appealing
- Why you think it was wrong

**What to expect:**
- We'll review your appeal
- We may ask for more info
- Our decision is final

**What can't be appealed:**
- CSAM violations
- Credible threats of violence
- Predatory behavior toward minors
- Repeated serious violations

## 8. Privacy During Moderation

### What We Monitor
All content on WeLynk may be scanned for policy violations, including:
- Messages
- Profile info and images
- Behavioral patterns

### Automated vs. Human
- **Most scanning is automated** - no human sees your content
- **Human review only happens** when content is flagged or reported

### Evidence Preservation
We may preserve and share content with law enforcement when:
- Required by law
- Necessary to prevent serious harm

For more details, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 9. Platform Immunity

WeLynk is a platform, not a publisher. Under Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act:
- Users are responsible for their own content
- We moderate in good faith to keep the platform safe
- We're not liable for user-generated content
