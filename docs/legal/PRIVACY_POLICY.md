## Our Promise

> **We will never sell your data.** Your messages, profile, and personal information are never sold to advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else.

This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. We've tried to write it in plain English.

## 1. What We Collect

> **TL;DR:** We collect what we need to run the service - your account info, messages, and basic usage data. We don't track your location.

### Account Information
- Email address (for permanent accounts)
- Username
- Age (required for safety features)
- Profile info you choose to add
- OAuth data if you sign in with Google/Apple

### Messages & Content
- Messages you send and receive
- Images you upload (profile pictures, etc.)
- Communication metadata (timestamps, who you talked to)

### Technical Data
- Device type and operating system
- IP address (for security, not location tracking)
- How you use the app (features, errors)
- General country (from IP address only)

### What We DON'T Collect
- GPS location or precise location data
- Your contacts (unless you explicitly share them)
- Data from other apps on your device
- Browsing history outside WeLynk

## 2. How We Use Your Data

> **TL;DR:** We use your data to run the service, keep you safe, and make WeLynk better. Never for ads.

### Running the Service
- Creating and managing your account
- Delivering your messages
- Matching you with compatible people
- Providing customer support

### Keeping You Safe
- Scanning content for harmful material
- Detecting harassment, scams, and threats

### Legal Requirements
- Complying with laws and court orders

## 3. How We Protect Your Data

> **TL;DR:** We use strong encryption, strict access controls, and industry-standard security practices.

### Encryption
- **Messages:** Encrypted with AES-256 (per-chat keys)
- **Data at rest:** All user data encrypted on our servers
- **Data in transit:** TLS encryption for all connections

### Access Controls
- Role-based access - employees only see what they need
- Audit logging for all data access
- Regular security assessments

### Important Note on Encryption

Our encryption is **server-side**, not end-to-end. This means:
- Your data is encrypted and protected from outside attackers
- WeLynk *can* access content when necessary (safety, legal compliance)
- This allows us to scan for harmful content and protect users

If you need end-to-end encryption where no one can ever access your messages, WeLynk isn't the right platform for you.

## 4. Message Privacy

> **TL;DR:** We don't read your messages. Automated systems scan for safety. Humans only review when there's a report or legal requirement.

### Automated Scanning
All messages go through automated safety systems that look for:
- Harmful content (harassment, threats, scams)
- Child safety violations
- Policy violations

This is automatic - no human reads your messages during normal scanning.

### When Humans May Review Messages
- When you or someone else reports a conversation
- When required by a court order or legal process
- When automated systems flag something serious
- To prevent imminent physical harm

### AI Bots vs. Human Conversations
- **Chats with humans:** Stay within WeLynk's systems, never shared externally
- **Chats with AI bots:** Processed by AI services to generate responses

Your conversations with real people are never sent to external AI providers.

## 5. Who We Share Data With

> **TL;DR:** We share limited data with service providers who help run WeLynk. We never sell your data.

### Service Providers
We work with companies that help us:
- Host our servers (cloud infrastructure)
- Deliver emails (account verification)
- Process images for safety (CSAM detection - required by law)
- Power AI bot conversations

All providers are bound by contracts that require them to protect your data and only use it for our services.

### Legal Requirements
We may share data when:
- Required by valid court orders or subpoenas
- Reporting CSAM to NCMEC (required by federal law)
- Preventing imminent physical harm

### What We NEVER Do
- Sell your data to anyone
- Share your messages with advertisers
- Use third-party tracking (no Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
- Give advertisers access to your profile or conversations

## 6. Your Data Rights

> **TL;DR:** You can access, correct, or delete your data. Some restrictions apply for safety and legal reasons.

### You Can:
- **Access your data** - Request a copy of what we have
- **Correct your data** - Fix inaccurate information
- **Delete your account** - Remove your data from our systems
- **Control your settings** - Manage privacy preferences
- **Appeal automated decisions** - Request human review of moderation actions

### How to Exercise Your Rights
Email admin@welynk.com with your request. We'll respond within 30 days.

### Deletion Limitations
We may delay deletion when:
- There's an active safety investigation
- Law enforcement requires data preservation
- Legal obligations require retention

CSAM reports are kept for 1 year minimum (federal law requirement).

## 10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you're a California resident, you have additional rights:

- **Right to Know:** What data we collect about you
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of your data
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Of data "sales" (we don't sell data anyway)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We won't treat you differently for exercising rights

We have NOT sold personal information in the past 12 months.

To exercise California rights: admin@welynk.com with subject "CCPA Request"
