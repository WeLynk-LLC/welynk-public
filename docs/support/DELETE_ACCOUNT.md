# How to Delete Your WeLynk Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your WeLynk account and what happens to your data.

## Delete Your Account on Web

1. Log in to your account at [welynk.com](https://welynk.com)
2. Click your profile picture and select **Settings**
3. Scroll down to the **Danger Zone** section
4. Click **Delete Account**
5. Type your exact username (case-sensitive) to confirm
6. Enter your password (if you signed up with email/password)
   - If you signed up with Google or Apple, this step is skipped
7. Click **Delete Account** to confirm

## Delete Your Account on Mobile

1. Open the WeLynk app
2. Tap your profile icon and select **Settings**
3. Scroll down to the **Danger Zone** section
4. Tap **Delete My Account**
5. Type your exact username (case-sensitive) to confirm
6. Enter your password (if you signed up with email/password)
   - If you signed up with Google or Apple, this step is skipped
7. Tap **Delete Account** to confirm

## What Happens to Your Data

### Data That Is Deleted

When you delete your account, all your personal information is removed:

- Your username, display name, and profile picture
- Your email address and password
- Your age and date of birth
- All active login sessions
- Your friend list and chat memberships

### Data That Is Retained

To comply with legal requirements and keep our community safe, we retain a limited amount of anonymized data:

| Data Type | Retention Period | Details |
|-----------|------------------|---------|
| Purchase history | 7 years | Required by law for tax purposes. Your personal details are removed—only transaction records are kept. |
| Safety records | As needed | If your account was involved in a safety report, anonymized records may be kept to help protect our community. |

**About your messages:** Messages you sent will still appear in your friends' conversations, but your name will be replaced with "Deleted User."

## Before You Go

- **This action cannot be undone.** Once deleted, your account and personal data cannot be recovered. If you're unsure, you can always take a break and come back later.
- **Cancel any subscriptions first.** If you have an active subscription, cancel it through Google Play or the App Store before deleting to avoid future charges.

## Need Help?

If you cannot delete your account or have questions:

- **Email:** support@welynk.com
